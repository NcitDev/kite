Kite — installing
=================

The quickest way is the "Install Kite.command" script in this window. It
copies Kite to Applications, clears the flag that stops it opening, launches
it, and closes itself.

DOUBLE-CLICKING IT WILL NOT WORK. macOS blocks downloaded scripts as well as
downloaded apps — a downloaded script that could switch off Gatekeeper would
make Gatekeeper pointless. To run it:

    1. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities, or search with Spotlight)
    2. Type:  bash          <- including the space after it
    3. Drag "Install Kite.command" into the Terminal window
    4. Press Return

That is the whole installation.


Why any of this is necessary
----------------------------

    "Kite" Not Opened
    Apple could not verify "Kite" is free of malware that may harm your Mac
    or compromise your privacy.

This is not a detection. macOS refuses to run any app that has not been
notarized by Apple, and notarization requires a paid Apple Developer ID that
this build does not have. The app is signed, but with an ad-hoc signature
carrying no identity Apple recognises.

You are trusting whoever gave you this file. That is a real decision, and no
amount of clicking makes it not one.


Doing it by hand instead
------------------------

1. Drag Kite onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Terminal, paste this line, and press Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kite.app

3. Open Kite normally.


Without Terminal at all
-----------------------

1. Drag Kite onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Double-click Kite and click Done on the warning. This failed attempt is
   required — the option below does not appear until macOS has blocked the
   app at least once.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll to Security.
4. Next to '"Kite" was blocked to protect your Mac', click Open Anyway.
5. Authenticate, then click Open Anyway again.

On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later, Control-clicking the app and choosing Open
no longer works. Apple removed that shortcut.
