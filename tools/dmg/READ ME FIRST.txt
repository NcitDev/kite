Kite — installing
=================

1. Drag Kite onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities, or search for it with Spotlight),
   paste this line, and press Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kite.app

3. Open Kite normally.

That is the whole installation. Only the first launch needs step 2.


Why this is necessary
---------------------

    "Kite" Not Opened
    Apple could not verify "Kite" is free of malware that may harm your Mac
    or compromise your privacy.

This is not a detection. macOS refuses to run any app that has not been
notarized by Apple, and notarization requires a paid Apple Developer ID that
this build does not have. The app is signed, but with an ad-hoc signature
carrying no identity Apple recognises.

You are trusting whoever gave you this file. That is a real decision, and no
amount of clicking makes it not one.


Why there is no button to click instead
---------------------------------------

macOS blocks downloaded scripts exactly as it blocks downloaded apps, so a
script shipped in this disk image would arrive carrying the same flag it is
meant to remove — double-clicking it fails the same way the app does.
Control-clicking the app and choosing Open does not work either: Apple
removed that shortcut in macOS 15 (Sequoia).

The Terminal line above is the shortest route that actually works.
